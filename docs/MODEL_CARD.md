# Model card

## Models

The frozen protocol fixes seven trained configurations and a majority-class rule. Each trained configuration is one
scikit-learn `Pipeline` (text cleaning → `TfidfVectorizer` → linear classifier) with a manifest that records its
configuration, training-set hash, artefact SHA-256 and library versions. Identifiers are the first 12 hexadecimal
characters of the artefact's SHA-256.

The historical model `9e34bdd2005c` has the same fitted parameters as the original two-file artefact `98bcdfb288fc`
(`results/historical/refit_equivalence.json`). `linsvc_bi_v2_full` was named the final model by the frozen protocol
from development accuracy alone, before any test score existed. The fitted binaries are not distributed; they are
regenerated by `python train.py` and `python -m study.fit` (see [`REPRODUCING.md`](REPRODUCING.md)).

## Intended use

Research baseline and teaching reference for binary polarity classification of English movie reviews of review
length; a fixed reference against which other classifiers can be compared on identical examples.

## Out of scope

Decisions about people; content moderation; domains other than film reviews; non-English, short or conversational
text; mixed or neutral opinions, which the training data excludes by construction (ratings 5–6 are absent);
probability estimates, since decision scores are uncalibrated and model-specific; inputs controlled by an adversary.

## Evaluation

Official IMDB test partition, 25,000 reviews (12,500 per class), scored once under the frozen protocol
(`protocol/protocol_v1.json`). Metrics are accuracy, macro F1 and class-conditional precision, recall and error, with
95% stratified block-bootstrap intervals (30-id blocks, 10,000 replicates). Results:
[`results/official_test/`](../results/official_test/); supplementary analyses: [`RESULTS.md`](RESULTS.md).
Efficiency measurements: [`BENCHMARKS.md`](BENCHMARKS.md).

## Known failure modes

- Bag-of-n-gram features carry no syntax, so negation, contrast and sarcasm reach the model only as co-occurring
  terms. On the held-out families of the behavioural suite, the trained configurations fail 81–89% of negation units
  and 75–88% of contrast units; these figures are provisional because the suite's labels are unadjudicated.
- Errors concentrate near the rating boundary: the final model misclassifies 21.4% of reviews rated 4 and 18.9% of
  reviews rated 7, against 4.7% at rating 1 and 6.0% at rating 10.
- Class-conditional error asymmetry depends on the configuration ([supplementary
  results](RESULTS.md#official-test-partition)).
- A review whose words are all out of vocabulary receives the sign of the intercept in batch scoring; the API returns
  422 `no_recognized_vocabulary` instead.

## Training data and attribution

Large Movie Review Dataset v1.0: Andrew L. Maas, Raymond E. Daly, Peter T. Pham, Dan Huang, Andrew Y. Ng and
Christopher Potts, "Learning Word Vectors for Sentiment Analysis", *Proceedings of ACL 2011*. The official training
partition (25,000 reviews) is used for fitting and development; the historical configurations use an 8,000-review
subset of it. The Hugging Face dataset card lists the licence as `other`. The repository's MIT licence covers its
code, not the review text.

## Behavioural suite provenance

The 237 constructed sentences in `behavioural/suite_v1.jsonl` and their expected labels were generated by
templates written by an AI assistant. No person has reviewed or adjudicated them; every case is marked
`unadjudicated`, and results on the suite are reported as provisional. `behavioural/annotation_queue_v1.csv` is the
queue for human review.

## LLM baseline (specified, not executed)

`study/llm/` implements a Claude baseline with prompt version `v1` (`study/llm/prompts/v1/`; prompt identity
`f13ab1b8a5cc…`, the SHA-256 over its three files' hashes that `study.llm.runner.load_prompt` records with every
request), structured JSON responses and resumable request records. No Claude model has been evaluated: no
spending limit has been set, so no API request has been made and no LLM result is reported. IMDB reviews are public
and widely redistributed, so any future LLM result carries an unquantified risk of training-data contamination.

## Ethical considerations

The reviews reflect the opinions and language of IMDB users writing before 2011. Vocabulary-based models can encode
associations between sentiment and named entities, genres or dialects. No fairness or subgroup analysis has been
performed.

## Serving contract

`POST /predict` takes `{"text": "..."}` and returns `label`, `sentiment` (0 or 1), `margin` and `model_version`.
`margin` is the served model's signed decision score $f(x) = \mathbf{w}^\top\phi(x) + b$ at full precision; despite
its name it is neither a geometric margin nor a probability. Guarantees, each tested in `tests/test_api.py`: one
`decision_function` call per request; the label follows the score with ties negative; 422 of type
`no_recognized_vocabulary` when the cleaned text has no in-vocabulary feature; 422 for empty, over-long (more than
20,000 characters) or letter-free input; 500 without internal details for unexpected errors. At startup the server
loads the artefact named by `models/serving.json` (or `$SENTIMENT_MODELS_DIR/serving.json`) and refuses to serve
unless the manifest's schema version, the artefact's SHA-256, the Pipeline's steps, cleaning version, classes and
feature dimension, and the scikit-learn version all match. `GET /model` returns the served artefact's manifest summary.
